# Sources of this data pack

- Leagues, divisions, clubs: openfootball (CC0 1.0, public domain), season 2025-26.
- First-tier squads: transfermarkt-datasets (github.com/dcaribou/transfermarkt-datasets), snapshot 2026-07-06;
  the data originates from Transfermarkt. Development / personal use; legal review needed before
  any public or commercial release.
- Second-tier players: generated, fictional names.
- Ratings: ESTIMATES from market value, age and league (see docs/REAL_DATA_PACK.md).
- No club badges, kit artwork or player photos are included.
