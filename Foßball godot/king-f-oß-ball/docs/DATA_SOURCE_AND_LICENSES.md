# Data sources and licences

| Data | Source | Licence / status | Real or generated |
|---|---|---|---|
| Leagues, divisions, clubs (names, structure) of England, Spain, Germany, Italy, France, season 2025-26 | openfootball (github.com/openfootball) | CC0 1.0 (public domain) | real |
| First-tier squads (names, positions, ages, nationality, market value) | transfermarkt-datasets (github.com/dcaribou/transfermarkt-datasets), snapshot 2026-07-06; data originates from Transfermarkt | published by its author; **development / personal use, legal review needed before any public or commercial release** | real (2,867 players, ids `tm_*`) |
| Second-tier players | generated in-game | fictional names | generated (ids `gen_*`) |
| Player ratings | estimated from market value, age and league strength | **estimates, not official ratings** | derived |
| Club colours, 3-letter codes | general knowledge (`tools/import_real/club_meta.json`) | no artwork | real, approximate |
| Countries (`data/countries.json`) | project data | project-owned | real names + 4 fictional regions |
| **Club crests** (`data/crests/crests.json`) | generated by `CrestGenerator` | original procedural designs; **not official, not affiliated with any club**; `ORIGINAL_NOT_AFFILIATED` (real clubs) / `FICTIONAL` | generated |
| Crest pictograms | `CrestSymbols` | original geometry | generated |
| Kits | colour/pattern data, rendered in-game | no official artwork | approximate |
| Player portraits | rendered in-game from stored appearance | no photographs | generated |
| User crests | imported by the player | `USER_SUPPLIED` (the player is responsible for rights) | user |

Rules in force: no official crest, kit artwork or player photo is included; real names and the Transfermarkt-derived data are NOT cleared for commercial use; every crest records its asset type, licence status and source; real-world verified records and generated records are separated by id prefix, `real` flag, `club_type` and `player_type`.
